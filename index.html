Good to know

What do I need to get started?

An iPhone and one clear photo of yourself. FitMe turns it into your avatar, and you can start trying outfits on as soon as it is ready.

Do I have to photograph my whole wardrobe?

No. Start with a few pieces, from a photo or a screenshot. FitMe removes the background and identifies each item, so adding more takes a moment.

Can I close the app while a look is being made?

Yes. Your look keeps generating after you leave, and FitMe can notify you when it is ready.

Is FitMe free?

FitMe is free to download. Try-ons use credits, and some features, such as the AI stylist, are part of FitMe Pro. Prices are shown in the app before you buy anything.

Which languages does FitMe speak?

The app is available in 27 languages.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are used to create your avatar and your looks. The details, including which services process them, are in the Privacy Policy.
