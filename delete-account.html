Delete your FitMe account

FitMe: AI Outfit Planner, by Summit Core Technologies. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open FitMe and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone.

Can't open the app? Request deletion by email

Email us from the address linked to your FitMe account, with the subject “Delete my FitMe account”:

info@summitcoretechnologies.com

We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record and sign-in link (email address or the identifier from Apple or Google).
  • Your profile: display name, username and profile photo.
  • The photos you uploaded and your avatar.
  • Your body and style details (such as gender, height, weight and body shape).
  • Your wardrobe items, generated try-on images and saved outfits.
  • Your stories, the reports you made and the reports about your content.
  • Your remaining credits.

What is kept

  • Story images are removed from view at once; their files are deleted from our servers within a few days.
  • Records of moderation decisions, which do not contain the removed image, may be kept to prevent repeated abuse.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and our payments partner (RevenueCat), which they keep under their own policies and as the law requires.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the store you bought it from: on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Delete some data without deleting your account

In the app you can remove a wardrobe item, a saved outfit, your avatar, a story or your profile photo at any time.
